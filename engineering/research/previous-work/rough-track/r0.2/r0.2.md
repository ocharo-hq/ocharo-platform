## Boundary

The developer requested continued scaffolding after the verified template foundation. This increment
makes preparation usable: operator → organization → brand → source media → draft → review → approved
manual package. The [preparation contract](../../../../architecture/preparation-contract.md) fixes the
authority, concurrency, storage, and HTTP boundaries shared by the implementation lanes.

Local operator accounts are the working default. Public registration, email delivery, provider
credentials, automated publishing, scheduling, and engagement measurement remain outside this slice.
The app owns membership independently of any future sign-in provider. Ocharo facts remain tenant data.
