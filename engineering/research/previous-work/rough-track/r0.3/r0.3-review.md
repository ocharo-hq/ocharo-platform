## 2. Review the catalogue editor

Open **Ocharo · current catalogue · UZ** to see the imported baseline, or
**Ocharo · catalogue editing review** to inspect a saved price/order/frame edit.
The starter chooser also includes Russian and English. Each starts from the actual current v22
catalogue: 16 pages, 29 garments, both one-piece and 10+-piece prices, and four photo collages.

1. Open a garment page. Choose the garment, change either price, and move it earlier/later within its page.
2. Reorder catalogue pages to choose the first category/spread; model identifiers remain stable.
3. Select a photo or collage frame. Drag/resize it or use exact position/size controls. Adjust crop and zoom.
4. Replace an image with a curated photo or a PNG/JPEG upload. Edit text, color, typography and layer order.
5. Use Undo/Redo, **Save design**, and **Save copy**. Reopen from saved designs and verify the changes.
6. Export the full PDF, current-page PNG, or editable JSON. Import the JSON to continue editing a separate draft.

Garment controls move the whole existing garment group between slots on the same page. Page controls reorder
entire spreads. Arbitrary garment transfers between category pages and a blank-catalogue builder are later work.
The editor preserves the source’s `S, M, L, XL, XXXL` and one-piece/10+-piece thresholds;
pricing for 2–9 pieces remains unspecified.

## 3. Review the business-card editor

Open **Ocharo · current card · UZ** for the source direction, or **Ocharo · card editing review**
for the saved ivory variant. The starter chooser includes three languages and labelled/logo-only fronts.

1. Switch Front/Back. Edit name, role, social handles, phone and location.
2. Compare Original v5, Ivory, Charcoal, Olive and Clay. Toggle the photographic background.
3. Select one of six logo colors or adjust its size/position. These preserve the original wordmark silhouette.
4. Edit text/frame styling with the same inspector, save a copy, reopen it and export both sides as PDF.
5. Export the selected face as PNG, or the complete editable document as JSON.

The existing v5 direction is the template source. The exact previously printed card variant/language was
not recorded, so this review does not claim to reproduce that unknown choice. New color presets are
editor alternatives awaiting preference, not changes to the approved brand identity.
