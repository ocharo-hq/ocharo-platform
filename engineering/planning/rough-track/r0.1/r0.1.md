## Boundary

This track follows the developer's initial commit and the native commit-status check described in
[operations](../../../operations/operations.md). Automatic commits additionally require effective ON
status from the user's exact directive. This track imports the inspected standard template
without replacing `.git` or these authored documents, adapts current conventions, and verifies the
API/UI, persistence, and worker foundation. It does not connect a social account or publish content.

The [template audit](../../../research/template-audit.md) owns technical gaps and the safe import
procedure. This whole-tree bootstrap stays in one coordinated lane; reusable SDK extraction follows
the shared development cycle after relevant behavior is proven.
