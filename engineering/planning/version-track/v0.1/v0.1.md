### Iteration 20 — Ocharo workspace frame

Owner decisions on 2026-10-17, from the [frame prototype](https://claude.ai/artifact/WWADnrV1w9uSBvKWVLsH7u):
a shared top bar for the whole Ocharo suite plus a sidebar per app; the logo, current app name and chevron form one
app button; the approved Ink and Raspberry / Neutral graphite palette becomes the single Ocharo theme with glass
chrome and the medium ambient gradient; on phones the app menu opens as a full-screen dialog. Desk is the first app;
the frame then rolls out to Catalogue, Studio, Marketing and Brand.

- [x] Ability to switch Desk sections without reloading the page
- [x] Ability to open any Ocharo app from the app button
- [ ] Ability to reach appearance, language, organization, version and sign-out from the account menu
- [ ] Ability to collapse the sidebar to an icon rail, remembered per browser
- [x] Ability to use Desk on a phone through a bottom bar and full-screen menus
- [x] Ability to follow the light, dark or system colour mode before the first paint
- [ ] Switch sections with unsaved edits → the discard prompt still guards the change
- [ ] Switch organization or sign out → the whole document still resets
- [ ] Review the frame on desktop and phone → owner confirms the layout

Verification on 2026-10-18: Desk adopted `@wow-two-beta/fe` 0.0.21, whose `ocharo` theme carries the approved palette
and medium ambient backdrop. Frontend lint, typecheck and 390 tests passed; the rebuilt Desk served on 8264. In the
browser, Events → Sourcing and back/forward kept the same document and renamed the tab; the phone width showed the
bottom bar and the full-screen app menu; desktop showed the grouped sidebar in light and dark. Not yet exercised:
the account menu, sidebar collapse, the unsaved-edit prompt on router moves and the organization switch; the router
guard has no dedicated test yet.
