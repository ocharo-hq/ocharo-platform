# Audience and adoption

*Last updated: 2026-09-26*

Ocharo is the first real organization. A second non-apparel fixture tests generality; it is not a
customer or evidence of market demand. Brand Workspace remains a working product name.

Assess usefulness through preparation time, review corrections, recovery effort, repeated voluntary
use, and qualified enquiries where attributable. Engagement does not establish causal sales impact.

Public SaaS acquisition, pricing, domain selection, branding, and billing follow validated operating
value. Existing Ocharo channels and business strategy remain in Maftuna's workspace; no external
marketing or account actions are part of the repository baseline.
