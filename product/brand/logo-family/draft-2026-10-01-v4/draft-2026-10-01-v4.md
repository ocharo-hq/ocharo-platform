## Result

The compact mark has a regenerated continuous needle shaft. One repaired master supplies the symbol,
tile, small icons and avatar. [Review the gallery](index.html).

The full wordmark and endorsement exports are byte-identical to revision 3. Their needle remains
part of the original approved artwork; it has not been separately resized or replaced.

## Source and repair

- `source/compact-master.png`: one generated repair of revision 3's compact symbol.
- `source/generation-prompt.txt`: exact edit instructions.
- `source/approved-master.png`: unchanged original full wordmark.
- `source/retained-v3-hashes.json`: expected hashes for unchanged wordmark exports and original source.
- `source/provenance.json`: generator, source inputs and lineage.

The original needle joins the script lettering. Revision 3's extraction left a notch in the lower
shaft. This revision reconstructs a smooth standalone taper. It is a generated derivative, not a
claim of exact original compact pixels. The oval, eye and overall direction remain visually related.

## Verification

`verification.json` records source identity, unchanged wordmark hashes, export dimensions,
matching alpha masks, favicon sizes and gallery layout checks.

The small eye becomes indistinct at 16 pixels. The exports preserve one consistent silhouette.

## Rebuild

Run `node build.cjs`, then `node render.cjs` with Playwright/Chromium available.
Set `OCHARO_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` when Playwright is outside normal module resolution.
Run `python3 verify.py` with Pillow installed for read-only export checks.
