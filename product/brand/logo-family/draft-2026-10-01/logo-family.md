# Ocharo logo family — review draft

*Last updated: 2026-10-01*

## Status

Eight generated PNG studies cover the principal placements: full signature, reverse signature, compact symbol, app tile, light avatar, small-size symbol in two treatments, and a `by Ocharo` endorsement.

Open [the review gallery](index.html) for side-by-side previews and downloads. These are review candidates, not a published Brand release. The original approved identity is the slanted Ocharo script with a tilted sewing needle integrated into the h. The new O-and-needle compact mark needs visual approval.

## What informed the set

The actual current WoW2 Panels exports include primary horizontal, symbol, lettering, rounded tile, and 16/24-pixel optical variants in navy and white. Wheelhouse also demonstrates a parent-brand endorsement. Ocharo uses that practical coverage while retaining its own script-and-needle identity. No shared logo conventions were analyzed or changed for this task.

For Ocharo, the full script already incorporates the needle: it serves as both the primary signature and wordmark. Adding a second adjacent needle symbol would duplicate the motif. The compact mark is reserved for constrained placements.

## Review findings and limits

- Wordmark spelling and the integrated needle are visible in the generated signatures.
- Independently generated treatments have contour and spacing differences. These are not deterministic recolors of one geometric master.
- Prompt colors were charcoal `#20211E`, white `#FFFFFF`, and ivory `#F7F3E9`. Generated pixels can vary; exact flat production colors are not certified.
- Two transparent white attempts produced visible speckling and were excluded. The selected reverse assets have opaque charcoal backgrounds.
- All delivered artwork is raster PNG. There is no editable vector master in this set.
- The small-size source is displayed at 16, 24, and 32 CSS pixels in the gallery. Those are browser simulations, not separately exported pixel-sized favicons.
- At 16 pixels, the compact outline remains more legible than the complete signature, but the needle eye is too small to communicate the sewing detail reliably.
- The slash-through-O silhouette can also read as Ø. Judge whether the needle eye is distinctive enough before adopting the compact mark.
- App corners and avatar circles are CSS masks in the gallery. Delivered square files retain their original pixels.
- The prior selected photographic social profile remains separate; this avatar is an alternative draft.

## Review decision

Approve or revise the compact O-and-needle direction alongside the existing script signature. After approval, derive a single clean master with matched light/dark geometry, exact colors, and separately verified 16/24/32-pixel exports before replacing app identities.

No application source, published Brand record, catalogue/card master, or social account was changed.
